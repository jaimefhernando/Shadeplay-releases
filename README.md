# Shadeplay

![Shadeplay](shadeplay-banner.png)

**Your game takes center stage. The rest of your setup adapts around it.**

By [Cypher Systems](https://cyphersystemsapps.dev/), an independent software studio from Chihuahua, Mexico.

When a game or video goes fullscreen, Shadeplay dims your other monitors so they stop pulling your
eyes away, then fades them back when you're done. Around that it can set your RGB lighting, lower
the monitors' real backlight, pause wallpaper apps, keep the cursor on the game, remind you to take
a break, and put everything back afterwards.

For Windows 10 and 11. In English, Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Русский and 简体中文.

## Download

Get **`Shadeplay-win-Setup.exe`** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and run it. It
installs for your user only (no admin rights needed), starts in the tray, and keeps itself up to
date from this page. A portable zip that runs without installing is on the same page.

Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC" the first time, because the installer isn't
code-signed yet. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Problems and ideas

[Open an issue](../../issues/new) and describe what happened. In the app, Settings › About and
updates › **Report a problem** saves a zip with the log, your settings (without passwords or keys)
and a list of your monitors that you can attach.

## Privacy

Shadeplay has no account and no telemetry. Your settings, play history and log stay on your PC
(`%AppData%\Shadeplay`). It only goes online to check this page for updates and, if you add your
own SteamGridDB key, to download game art by name.
